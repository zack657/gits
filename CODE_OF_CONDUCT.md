# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still addressing real problems.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and user impact.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's credentials, keys, private repository paths, or personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after maintainers ask for it to stop.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of credentials, SSH keys, or user data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants when behavior harms the project or community.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
